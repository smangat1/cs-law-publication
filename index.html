About

HB tracks the legal edges of technical systems without pretending neutrality is the same thing as clarity.

It is a small publication for pressure points: governance, procedure, discretion, institutional design, and the way technical defaults start behaving like public rules.

The tone is formal enough to take the work seriously and loose enough to stay curious.
