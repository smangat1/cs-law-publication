Editorial Note

HB is a small publication for the places where technical systems start behaving like institutions.

It reads for governance, procedure, pressure points, discretion, and the quiet moments when design choices begin acting like public rules.

Mission

HB is formal enough to take the work seriously and loose enough to stay curious. It is not interested in pretending neutrality is the same thing as clarity. The publication is built for arguments, essays, notes, and larger issue-based packages that try to say something precise about law in technical environments.

What belongs here

Issues as curated editorial packages Articles as tighter, sharper interventions Essays as longer sustained arguments
